## 🕹️ About

The Guild 1 Remake: Europa 1410 is a medieval economic and political strategy simulation developed by Ashborne Games and published by THQ Nordic. Set in 15th-century Europe, you build a business, expand your dynasty, navigate city politics, and pursue influence across generations. The game is planned for Windows Early Access on September 17, 2026.

Designed for players who enjoy deep medieval management, economic strategy, political maneuvering, and long-term dynasty building.

> ⚠️ **This is an unofficial leaked pre-release build.** The game is under active development: expect bugs, unfinished content, and balance changes.Archive is password protected (`2026`) to compatibility automated DMCA bots and hosting service scanners that would otherwise delete the files within hours.

## ✨ Features

- 🏰 **Medieval Dynasty** — Grow a family legacy and secure your succession across generations.
- ⚒️ **Craft and Trade** — Choose a profession, manage production, and expand your commercial empire.
- 💰 **Economic Strategy** — Build wealth through business decisions, trade, and resource management.
- 🏛️ **City Politics** — Climb the social ladder and compete for powerful civic offices.
- 🗡️ **Crime and Law** — Pursue criminal schemes or protect the city and uphold public order.
- 🤝 **Influence and Rivalry** — Use alliances, diplomacy, popularity, and cunning to outmanoeuvre rivals.
